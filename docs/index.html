BookLocal Guide

Travel with someone who actually lives there.

BookLocal Guide connects travellers with independent local guides. Browse guides in the city you are visiting, message them, agree on what you want to see, and book — with the payment held safely until the trip is done.

For travellers

  • Find guides by city, language and speciality.
  • Message before you commit — text, voice notes and shared locations.
  • Your card is authorised, not charged. The guide is paid only after you confirm the trip went ahead.
  • Cancel free up to 24 hours before.
  • Report or block anyone, from the conversation itself.

For guides

  • Set your own services, prices, working days and days off.
  • Get paid straight to your bank account through Stripe.
  • No booking can land outside the hours you set.

Safety

Every conversation can be reported and every user blocked. Payments stay on the platform so there is something to fall back on if a trip goes wrong, and our team reviews reports within 24 hours.
