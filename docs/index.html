BookLocal Guide

Travel with someone who actually lives there.

BookLocal Guide connects travellers with independent local guides. Browse guides in the city you are visiting, message them, agree on what you want to see, and send a booking request — free, with no commission and no card details.

For travellers

  • Find guides by city, language and speciality.
  • Message before you commit — text, voice notes and shared locations.
  • Completely free. You pay the guide directly on the day, at their own rate.
  • Cancel any time before the trip.
  • Report or block anyone, from the conversation itself.

For guides

  • Set your own services, prices, working days and days off.
  • Keep 100% of what travellers pay you — we take no commission.
  • No booking can land outside the hours you set.

Safety

Every conversation can be reported and every user blocked, from the chat itself. First contact stays in the app, so reviews and reports follow people around rather than disappearing into a private message. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.
