Common questions

When am I actually charged?

Your card is authorised when you book, but the money is only taken after the trip and after you confirm it went ahead. If you neither confirm nor report a problem within 24 hours of the guide marking the trip finished, it is released automatically.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open Bookings, choose the booking and tap Cancel Booking. Cancelling is free up to 24 hours before the start time and the authorisation on your card is released.

Something went wrong on the tour

In Bookings, tap Report a Problem before confirming completion. The payment stays held while we look into it, and you can message our team from the support chat on that booking.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the conversation and tap the avatar in the top right. You can report the person, block them, or both. Blocking stops all messages and bookings between you. Undo it in Profile → Blocked Users.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, chats and messages. Complete or cancel any active booking first. Email us if you would rather we did it for you.

I am a guide and nobody can book me

Two things have to be done first:

  • Availability — set your working days and hours.
  • Connect payouts — finish Stripe's verification. Until Stripe confirms your account can receive money, your profile shows as not ready for bookings. Verification can take a little while after you finish the form.

Why can't I send a phone number or Instagram handle in chat?

Bookings and payments have to stay on the platform — that is what lets us hold the money safely and step in if there is a dispute. Sharing contact details in chat is blocked, and repeated attempts lead to a temporary mute.
