Common questions

How do I pay?

You pay the guide directly, on the day — in cash or however you agree together. BookLocal Guide never takes your card details and takes no commission. Agree the price in the chat before you meet, so you both have a record.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open Bookings, choose the booking and tap Cancel Booking. Nothing is charged. Please cancel as early as you can — a last-minute cancellation costs the guide their day.

Something went wrong on the tour

Leave an honest review, and report the guide from the chat if they behaved badly. Because we never held your money we cannot refund it, but we do act on reports and will remove guides who mistreat travellers.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the conversation and tap the avatar in the top right. You can report the person, block them, or both. Blocking stops all messages and bookings between you. Undo it in Profile → Blocked Users.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, chats and messages. Complete or cancel any active booking first. Email us if you would rather we did it for you.

I am a guide and I can't reply to anyone

Replying needs an active subscription. Travellers can always find you and message you for free, and you can read everything they send — but answering is the part the subscription pays for. Open your dashboard and tap See plans. Billing goes through your Apple ID, and you can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

I subscribed on another device

Open the plans screen and tap Restore in the top right. That asks Apple for any subscription tied to your Apple ID.

How do travellers find me?

Set your working days and hours and add at least one service with a price. Without them you can still be found, but travellers see a default rate you never chose and can request any hour of any day.

Why can't I send a phone number or Instagram handle in chat?

First contact has to happen here. Once a conversation moves to a private number, reviews, reports and blocks stop meaning anything — a guide who mistreats someone can just start again. Keeping the introduction in the app is what makes the safety tools work. Sharing contact details is blocked in chat and in profiles, and repeated attempts lead to a temporary mute.
