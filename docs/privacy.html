BookLocal Guide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

BookLocal Guide connects travellers with local guides. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can get it deleted.

Who we are

BookLocal Guide is operated by Asadulloh ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights below, contact asadulloh5236@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 30 days.

What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Name (from Sign in with Apple, or you type it) So guides and travellers know who they are talking to
Email address Account identity and account-related messages. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address
Phone number (optional) Contacting you about a booking
Profile photo (optional) Shown on your profile
Guide profile: city, country, languages, specialities, bio, experience, prices, working hours Shown publicly so travellers can find and book you
Messages: text, voice notes, locations you choose to share Delivering the conversation, and investigating reports of abuse
Bookings: date, number of people, notes, amount, status Running the booking and the payment
Reviews and ratings Shown publicly on the guide's profile
Reports and blocks you submit Safety and moderation
Push notification token Sending you message and booking notifications

What we never collect

We do not store card numbers. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; card details go from your device to Stripe and never reach our servers.

We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the processors that make the app work:

ServiceWhat it handles
Google Firebase Sign-in, database, push notifications
Stripe Payments and guide payouts. Guides complete Stripe's own onboarding and provide their identity and bank details directly to Stripe
Supabase Storage for profile photos and voice messages
Railway Hosting for our server

We also share what is necessary between the two sides of a booking: a guide sees the traveller's name, photo and booking details, and a traveller sees the guide's public profile. Contact details are not shared automatically.

We may disclose data if we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud or protect someone's safety.

Where your data is held

Our providers store data on servers in the European Union and the United States. By using the app you accept that your data is transferred to and processed in those countries.

How long we keep it

  • Account data — until you delete your account.
  • Messages — until either participant's account is deleted.
  • Bookings and payment records — kept after account deletion where tax and accounting law requires it, and to resolve disputes.
  • Reports — kept after account deletion, so a banned user cannot erase their history by deleting and re-registering.

Your rights

Delete your account. Open the app, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, your chats and your messages. You will be asked to complete or cancel any active booking first. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

You can also ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or restrict how we use it. Most details you can edit yourself in the app under Profile.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (bookings, payments, messaging), our legitimate interest (safety, moderation, fraud prevention), and your consent (push notifications, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

Age

BookLocal Guide involves payments and arranging to meet people in person. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Contact details are stored separately from public profiles so that browsing the app never exposes an email or phone number. Database access is restricted by server-side rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we will notify affected users and the relevant authority if a breach puts your data at risk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
