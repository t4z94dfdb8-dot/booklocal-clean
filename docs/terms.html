3. Bookings and payment

BookLocal Guide does not handle payment for tours. When you book, you are sending the guide a request. They confirm or decline it, and you pay them directly — in person, in cash or by whatever method you agree between you.

We never take your card details for a tour, never hold your money, and never take a cut of what you pay the guide. The price shown in the app is the guide's own rate, for information.

Because the payment is between you and the guide, any dispute about money is too. We can suspend accounts and remove profiles, but we cannot refund a tour we were never paid for. Agree the price in the chat before you meet, so there is a record.

Cancellations

Either side can cancel a booking in the app before it happens. Nothing is charged, because nothing was taken. Please cancel as early as you can — a last-minute cancellation costs the other person their day.
